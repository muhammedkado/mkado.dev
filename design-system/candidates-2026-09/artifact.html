mkado.dev · portfolio redesign · 10 candidates

Ten directions for the same seven applications

Every page below shows the same facts: the claim, the seven live demos with their status dots and one-click sign-ins, how the site is run, the USTEK years, skills with evidence, the notes, and a contact form. What changes is the decision: layout, type, canvas and motion. Pick one and I build it across the whole site, in English and Turkish, with the live status probe and the contact form wired in.

  • Open each design and use the pill in the bottom-right corner to switch dark and light.
  • Resize the window, or open a page on your phone. The small previews are full-length phone captures.
  • Screenshots are the real demos. Copy buttons, forms and status dots are live in the built site, static here.
Light Dark
01

Signal Index

editorial · precise · warm
Layout
Numbered index of the seven apps; hover previews in a sticky panel on wide screens, rows expand on tap
Canvas
Warm paper / ink night, cobalt as the only colour
Type
Fraunces (soft serif, optical sizes) + Schibsted Grotesk
Motion
Headline words rise in sequence, row rules draw on hover, clip-reveal previews

You read like a considered engineer who writes things down: calm, precise, no gimmicks. The most résumé-like of the ten while still having a voice.

Control Room

telemetry · calm · dark-first
Layout
Split work section: project info pinned on the left while the seven screenshots stream past on the right
Canvas
Graphite / cool white; green is reserved for "up"
Type
Sora + Martian Mono readouts
Motion
Pulse ring, live clock, pinned panel crossfades in sync with the scrolling media

You run production systems and you are comfortable with the ops side. Reads as a backend engineer who ships and monitors, not a designer who codes.

Swiss Ledger

grid · objective · résumé
Layout
Strict 12-column grid; the work is a real table with a thumbnail strip, demo accounts are a ledger
Canvas
White / black, one red rule
Type
Host Grotesk only, three weights
Motion
Rules draw in, red nav marker, nothing else

You are rigorous and you respect the reader’s time. The closest thing to a printed CV that still feels designed. Recruiters will love it; it takes no risks.

Gallery Wall

A tight-guttered wall of the seven screenshots at mixed sizes; captions slide up on hover, everything else is short bands below

Atelier

Monolith
